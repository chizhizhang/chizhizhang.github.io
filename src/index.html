Blue laser · Pseudo datasetCSV

450 nm blue laser pseudo dataset

18trajectories

Based on original measurements.For testing and benchmarking under simulated conditions only.

This pseudo dataset contains 18 generated degradation trajectories for 450 nm blue laser diodes, sampled every 0.5 h. Power and voltage trends are constrained by original measurements. Device-specific aging-current changes after 1500 h are retained, with smooth changes in degradation rate while the recorded aging temperature stays constant.

Attribution: Chizhi Chris Zhang; Changchun Institute of Optics, Fine Mechanics and Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences; China Electronic Product Reliability and Environmental Testing Research Institute (CEPREI; the Fifth Electronics Research Institute of MIIT), Guangzhou

Blue laser · Pseudo datasetCSV

455 nm blue laser pseudo dataset

20trajectories

Based on original measurements.For testing and benchmarking under simulated conditions only.

This pseudo dataset contains 20 generated blue-laser degradation trajectories at 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, and 4.5 A. The source tests used a 25 °C water-cooling setpoint. Each trajectory covers 0–500 h at 0.5 h intervals, with optical power, current, voltage, wavelength, and bandwidth. Low-output spectral fluctuations follow the patterns in the original instrument records.

Attribution: Chizhi Chris Zhang; Changchun Institute of Optics, Fine Mechanics and Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences; China Electronic Product Reliability and Environmental Testing Research Institute (CEPREI; the Fifth Electronics Research Institute of MIIT), Guangzhou

Files & variables +

Files

One CSV per pseudo device. Trajectories cover 0–500 h; records that do not reach the chosen failure threshold by 500 h are right censored. Use the 20 h optical-power value as the reference for normalized-power analyses. Large wavelength and bandwidth fluctuations at very low optical output represent instrument-like tail readings.
